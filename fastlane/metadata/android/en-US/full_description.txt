pugdon is an app for Mastodon and the fediverse, made with care and with a one-eyed pug for a mascot. 🐾

Sign in to your Mastodon server and follow everything in an app that's pretty, fast and built to be accessible.

🏠 TIMELINES YOUR WAY
• Home, Local and Federated, plus feeds pinned at the top
• Trending: popular posts on your server and across the fediverse
• Hashtag feeds: mix several hashtags into one feed and leave others out
• Lists: create, edit and follow your lists, or start one from a profile
• Other servers: read another community's local timeline without an account there
• Home keeps your place when you switch tabs

🔎 SEARCH AND EXPLORE
• Find people, hashtags and posts, including from other servers
• Explore what's trending: posts, hashtags, people and news

✍️ WRITING
• Images, GIFs and videos, from the gallery or the camera
• Crop, rotate and flip images, with focal points so crops show what matters
• Image descriptions (alt text), with a pug that reminds you to write them
• Polls, content warnings, custom emoji, visibility and language
• Mention and emoji suggestions while you type
• Drafts, scheduled posts, and editing or deleting what you posted
• Quote posts: quote others and choose who can quote you
• Share links and pictures from other apps straight into a new post

🔔 NOTIFICATIONS
• Grouped or one by one, under Today and Earlier
• Direct messages grouped by person

💬 AND MORE
• Profiles with fields, pinned posts, a media grid, followers and following
• See who favourited or boosted, save media to your phone
• Bookmarks, several accounts in one app
• Report, mute and block
• Hide the numbers on posts if you'd rather not see them
• Nine color coats, light and dark mode, and true black
• In English and Portuguese

🔒 PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking, no analytics. pugdon talks only to your Mastodon server. This build has no Google code, so push notifications aren't in it yet: open the Notifications tab to see what's new.

pugdon is free software (MIT license). The code is at github.com/cajuuh/pugdomv2, and ideas and bug reports are welcome there.
